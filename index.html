About Me

I am a master's student in Civil Engineering, specializing in Geotechnical Engineering, at the University of Colorado Boulder.

I received my undergraduate education in Geophysics from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), China.

My research interests lie at the intersection of rock mechanics, computational geomechanics, and geophysics, with a particular focus on rock deformation, fracture, and thermo-mechanical damage.

Current Research

Wildfire-Induced Rock Spalling

My current research focuses on numerical modeling of wildfire-induced rock spalling, investigating thermo-mechanical damage, rock fracture, and surface evolution under repeated thermal loading.

I use finite element modeling to study rock deformation and damage evolution under coupled thermal and mechanical processes.
